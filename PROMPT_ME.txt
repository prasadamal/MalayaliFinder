MALAYALIFINDER — PROMPT ME
===========================
Last updated: 23 November 2026. Founder: Amal Prasad (company Ariyoo). Status: PARKED.

THE IDEA
"Find your Mallu tribe": a Flutter app (Android/iOS) to help Malayalis find each other anywhere
in the world — an animated radar of nearby Malayalis, a map, and an event planner (food, sports,
cultural, music, travel meetups).

WHERE IT STANDS (from the 16 Nov portfolio analysis)
- UI prototype from a single commit in March. The radar's people are generated with Random()
  around your location and events are hard-coded. No backend, no accounts, no tests, no CI.
- A live radar of strangers by community raises real safety and store-policy problems.

WHAT IS WORTH KEEPING / THE VIABLE END PRODUCT
- The audience: the Malayali diaspora (Gulf, Bangalore, UK, US) is large, organised and
  underserved, and the founder understands it.
- The viable product is events and associations (Onam gatherings, food meetups, a directory),
  NOT a radar of people. Cheapest first test: a shareable Kerala quiz web page.
- ReelMates' Malayalam cinema collection already reaches the same audience.

ON HOLD
Everything. Rule from the analysis: no new product until one of the live apps (Forward,
ReelMates, Wayfare, Ariyoo) has 500 weekly users. Other apps: see
github.com/prasadamal/ariyoo-clone PROMPT_ME.txt.
